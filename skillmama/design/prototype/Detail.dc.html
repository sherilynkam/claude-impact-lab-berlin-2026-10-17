Eye-hand coordination5–6 mo+

Basket with known objects

A first, gentle experience of choice: Mila picks the toy she wants.

Set up
1Put 2 or 3 of her favourite toys in a small soft basket.
2Lay or sit her next to it and let her reach in and choose.
3Swap toys in as her favourites change.
Watch for
Which toy she reaches for first, and whether she uses one hand or two.
Check toys for small parts and sharp edges. Stay close.
