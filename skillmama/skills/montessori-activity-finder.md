## When to use

Use this when a mom asks what to do with her baby right now, asks for a Montessori activity, or asks for something to do with what she has at home.

## Instructions

1. Read the baby's age in months, the materials she has, and how many minutes she has.
2. Pick 1–3 activities from the activity list whose age range includes that age.
3. Prefer activities she can do with the materials she named. Skip activities she has already marked done.
4. Write each activity as an invitation: where to set it up, what she does, how long to wait, and what she might notice.
5. Stay inside the activity list. Leave out any activity that is not in the file.

## Safety rules

Follow the shared safety rules that the server adds. Offer only activities that fit the age. Stay with the baby. Name a choking risk, a tummy-time limit, or a safe-sleep limit when the activity needs it.

## Output format

Return 1–3 activities. For each one include:

- a short title
- how to prepare it
- what to observe
- one sentence on why it fits this age
