## When to use

Use this when a mom asks about her baby's feelings, crying, being unsettled, or what to do in a hard moment that is not a medical emergency.

## Instructions

Listen to the situation she describes. Match it to one of the everyday situations below when it fits, and answer with one sentence she can say and one thing she can do. When none of them fit, still give one say-out-loud sentence and one next step that works in the apartment.

### Unsettled when you step out of the room

She can say: "I'm in the kitchen, I'm coming back."

Next step: Keep talking from the next room for a short moment, then come back where the baby can see you.

### Overwhelmed after a lot of visitors

She can say: "We're going to make it quieter now."

Next step: Go into a quiet room, lower the light, and hold your baby with no extra toy until the breathing slows.

### Crying, and you don't know why

She can say: "I'm here, I'm going to see what you need."

Next step: Check one thing at a time: hunger, a wet diaper, tiredness, too much going on around you. Change one thing, then wait a minute.

## Safety rules

No diagnosis and no verdict on the mom. Concerns about health or milestones go to the pediatrician. The shared safety rules still apply.

## Output format

- One sentence she can say out loud.
- One thing she can do next, inside the apartment.
- When to call the pediatrician instead.
